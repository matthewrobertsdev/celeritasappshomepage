Light Joy

Decorate your screen with lights all year round or for holidays or special occasions!

Decorate your screen with colorful lights, both all year round and with special themes for holidays and special occasions! Single color, custom color, changing, and holiday and special-occasion themes are available, as well as various bulb shapes, on/off modes for the lights, various cord colors, float-on-top mode or not, and ways of showing and hiding different strands of lights. Lights will appear on all your displays for your computer. You can use your computer as usual with the lights around the edges as a decoration. In-app purchase is required to use all themes besides rainbow.

Note: does not go over other apps that are in fullscreen mode.
